Sign in to Google

Your vehicles are stored as vehicledata/vehicles.json in your Google Drive, with the photos alongside it in vehicledata/photos. This app uses Drive's restricted drive.file permission, so it can only ever see the folder it created — nothing else in your Drive.

Setup needed

Open config.js and paste your Google OAuth client ID into GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID. Step-by-step instructions are in README.md.
